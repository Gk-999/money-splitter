A free Splitwise alternative with no signup

Updated 12 November 2026

Splitwise is a popular, well-established app for sharing expenses. But sometimes you just want to split a trip or a dinner right now, without everyone creating an account or installing an app. That's what PantherSplits is for: open the page, split the bill, send the result on WhatsApp.

In short: choose PantherSplits for quick, private splits with no signup, including a shared pot (kitty) mode. Choose Splitwise if your group wants balances synced to everyone's account and tracked over many months.

Why people choose PantherSplits

  • No signup for anyone. One person does the split; nobody else needs anything installed.
  • Private by default. Your expenses stay in your own browser. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Shared pot mode. When everyone chips in up front and one person holds the money, it works out refunds or top-ups automatically. See how it works.
  • Built for WhatsApp groups. One tap sends the full summary: totals, groups and who pays whom.
  • Fast to use. Payer chips, Enter-to-add, and one-tap "same people, new split" for regular groups.

When Splitwise is the better fit

  • Your group wants everyone to see and edit the same balances from their own phone.
  • You want running totals across many months and many groups in one place.
  • You regularly split in several currencies.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free alternative to Splitwise without signing up?

Yes. PantherSplits is free and runs in your web browser with no account and no app to install. Open the page, add people and expenses, and share the result on WhatsApp.

Does everyone in the group need the app?

No. One person can do the split and send the summary on WhatsApp, or share a link that opens the same split in anyone's browser.

When is Splitwise the better choice?

Splitwise suits groups that want balances synced to everyone's account across devices, running totals over many months, or multiple currencies. PantherSplits suits one-off and recurring splits where you want no signup and your data kept on your own device.
