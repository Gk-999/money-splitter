Trip expense calculator: split group travel costs fairly

Updated 12 November 2026

On a group trip, different people pay for different things, and not everyone joins every activity. PantherSplits is a free trip expense calculator that splits each cost only among the people who shared it, then tells you the fewest payments needed to settle up. It runs in your browser, with no signup.

Quick answer: list every expense with who paid and who shared it, divide each one among its sharers, add up each person's portions, and subtract what they paid. Positive means they get money back; negative means they owe. Then match the people who owe with the people who are owed.

How to split a trip with PantherSplits

  1. Add everyone on the trip. Type a name, press Enter, type the next.
  2. Log each expense as it happens. Enter what it was and the amount, tap who paid, and untick anyone who wasn't part of it.
  3. Check the groups. Expenses are grouped by who shared them, with the amount each person pays, so "Everyone except Divya ₹2,400 · ₹800 each" is easy to verify.
  4. Settle and share. Send the summary to the trip's WhatsApp group and tick each payment once it's done.

Tips for splitting travel costs

  • Log expenses the same day. Small cash costs like tolls and tea are the ones people forget.
  • Collect a kitty for shared basics. If everyone agrees to chip in up front, switch to Shared Pot mode and let one person hold the money.
  • Don't split what wasn't shared. Splitting only among the people involved avoids the most common arguments.
  • Settle before the photos are shared. Sending the summary while the trip is fresh gets payments done faster.

Frequently asked questions

How do you split trip expenses when not everyone shared every item?

Split each expense only among the people who shared it. Each person's fair share is the total of their portions across all items. Compare that with what they actually paid: the difference is what they owe or get back.

What is the fewest number of payments needed to settle a trip?

At most one fewer than the number of people. PantherSplits matches the people who owe with the people who are owed, so a group of four never needs more than three payments, and often fewer.

Should one person pay for everything on a trip?

It is simpler but not required. If everyone agrees to chip in a fixed amount first, use Shared Pot mode and let one person hold the money. If people pay as they go, use Split mode and record who paid each expense.
