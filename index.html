A free bill splitter for trips, flatmates and group outings

PantherSplits is a free, no-signup expense splitter that runs entirely in your browser. Add the people, log what was spent, and it works out the fewest payments needed to settle up. Then send the whole summary to your group on WhatsApp in one tap.

Two ways to split

Split — pay as you go

For when different people pay for different things. Record who paid for each expense and who it was for. Items shared by only some people are divided only among them.

Example: Asha pays ₹900 for a cab for Asha, Ben and Chetan (₹300 each). Ben pays ₹300 for snacks for Ben and Chetan (₹150 each). Asha is owed ₹600, so Ben pays Asha ₹150 and Chetan pays Asha ₹450.

Shared Pot — chip in first

For when everyone puts in a fixed amount up front and one person holds the money and pays for things. PantherSplits reconciles the pot at the end, whether it was underspent or overspent.

Example: Four friends put in ₹500 each (₹2,000) and Raj holds the pot. They spend ₹1,600, leaving ₹400, so Raj refunds ₹100 to each of the other three. If they had spent ₹2,400 instead, each of the other three would pay Raj ₹100.

How to split expenses with PantherSplits

  1. Add people. Type each name and press Enter. In Shared Pot mode, set how much everyone puts in and mark who holds the pot.
  2. Log expenses. Enter what it was and the amount, tap who paid (in Split mode) and who it was for.
  3. Check the balances. Expenses are grouped by who shared them, with the amount each person pays. Tap anyone's name to see exactly which items make up their share.
  4. Settle up and share. Follow the suggested payments, tick each one when it's paid, and share the summary on WhatsApp or as a link.

Frequently asked questions

Is PantherSplits free?

Yes. PantherSplits is completely free to use. There is no account, no signup and no subscription.

Do I need to download an app or create an account?

No. It runs in any modern web browser on your phone or computer. Just open the page and start adding people and expenses.

Where is my data stored?

Only in your own browser, on your device. Nothing is uploaded to a server. When you share a link, the whole project is packed into the link itself, so share it only with people you trust.

How does PantherSplits work out who owes whom?

It adds up what each person paid and what each person's fair share is, then suggests the fewest payments needed to make everyone even.

What is Shared Pot mode?

Use Shared Pot when everyone puts money in first, for example ₹500 each, and one person holds the pot and pays for things. PantherSplits then tells you how much the holder should refund if money is left over, or how much everyone should top up if the pot was overspent.

Can I split an expense between only some people?

Yes. In both modes you can choose who each expense was for, so an item only a few people shared is divided only among them. Expenses are grouped by who shared them, with the amount each person pays.

Can I start a new split with the same group?

Yes. Tap New split and choose Same people, clear expenses. Your group stays, the expenses are cleared, and you can undo it for a few seconds.

How do I share the result with my group?

Tap WhatsApp to send a full summary of expenses and who pays whom, or tap Copy link to share a link that opens the same split in anyone's browser.
