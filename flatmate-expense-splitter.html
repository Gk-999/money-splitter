Flatmate expense splitter: rent, bills and groceries

Updated 12 November 2026

Sharing a flat means one person pays the landlord, another pays the electricity, and groceries get bought by whoever is at the shop. PantherSplits keeps track of who paid what, splits each bill among the right flatmates, and tells you the fewest payments to square up each month. Free, no signup.

Quick answer: record each bill with who paid it and which flatmates share it. Each flatmate's share is their portion of every bill they're on; subtract what they paid to see who owes whom. Start a fresh split each month with the same people.

Different room sizes

If the rooms aren't equal, agree each person's portion of the rent first. Then enter the rent as one expense per person instead of one big bill. For example, with rent of ₹36,000 paid by Riya:

  • "Rent — Riya's room" ₹14,000, shared by Riya only
  • "Rent — Sam's room" ₹11,000, shared by Sam only
  • "Rent — Tanvi's room" ₹11,000, shared by Tanvi only

Each portion then lands with the right flatmate, and everything else stays split normally.

A monthly routine that works

  1. Log bills as they're paid through the month. Payer chips remember the last person who paid, so repeat bills are quick.
  2. Untick anyone who isn't part of a bill, like groceries for people who cook.
  3. Settle at month end. Share the summary in the flat's WhatsApp group and tick each payment as it arrives.
  4. Start next month with New split → Same people, clear expenses.

Prefer a common household fund instead? Everyone can chip in monthly and one person pays the bills — that's the shared pot approach.

Frequently asked questions

How do you split rent when rooms are different sizes?

Agree each person's portion of the rent first, then enter the rent as one expense per person: for example "Rent — Sam's room ₹11,000" paid by whoever paid the landlord and shared only by Sam. Each portion then lands with the right person.

How do flatmates split groceries if someone doesn't eat at home?

Split each grocery bill only among the flatmates who share the food. Anyone who opts out is simply left off that expense, so they don't pay for it.

How do I start a new month with the same flatmates?

Tap New split and choose Same people, clear expenses. Everyone stays in the group and the month's expenses start from zero. You can undo it for a few seconds if you tap it by mistake.
